2016-04-13
Place of Meeting Tour participants are asked to bring a passport and to arrive at the meeting place for Cheong Wa Dae tours located in the parking lot on the east side of Gyeongbok Palace 30 minutes prior to the time reserved. Please arrive at the Cheong Wa Dae Tour Information Booth, located in the East Gate Parking Lot of Gyeongbokgung Palace, at least 30 minutes before the start of the tour. After the tour guide checks IDs, your group will board the shuttle and proceed to Cheong Wa Dae. Please bring your passport with you for the ID check. Upon arrival, there will be a brief security check followed by a short introductory video. The actual tour will begin shortly after and cover areas such as Nokjiwon Garden, the old building grounds, the Main Building and Yeongbin-gwan (the State Guest House).
